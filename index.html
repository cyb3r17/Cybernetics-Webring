How to join

The ring is open to personal sites, art projects, zines, and machines that feel like a place. To be added, open an issue with your name, url, and a one-line description:

» github.com/cyb3r17/Cybernetics-Webring/issues

Then paste the badge below onto your site so the ring stays whole.
